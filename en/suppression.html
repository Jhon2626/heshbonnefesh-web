Account Deletion

App: Heshbon Nefesh · Developer: Yonathan Lalou — Ashdod, Israel · contact@heshbonnefesh.app

This page explains how to request deletion of your Heshbon Nefesh account and its associated data, and which data is deleted or retained.

1 From the app

Account deletion is done directly inside the Heshbon Nefesh app:

  • Open the Heshbon Nefesh app on your phone.
  • Go to Profile (or Settings).
  • Open the Account section, then tap Delete my account.
  • Confirm. Deletion is permanent and irreversible.

2 By email

If you no longer have access to the app, email contact@heshbonnefesh.app from your account email address, with the subject "Account Deletion". We process requests within 30 days.

3 Data that is deleted

Deleting your account permanently erases:

  • your email address and account identifiers;
  • your spiritual practice data (actions, challenges, feelings, personal notes, reviews);
  • your location data and saved preferences.

4 Data that is retained

We keep no practice data after deletion. We do not store your payment details: subscription and payment records are handled by the platforms (Google Play, Apple, RevenueCat) and may be retained by those providers to meet their legal and accounting obligations, independently of your account deletion.

5 Timing

Your personal and practice data is removed immediately from our active systems, and within 30 days at the latest. Residual backup copies are purged within a maximum of 90 days.

6 Contact

For any question about deleting your account: contact@heshbonnefesh.app.
